Implementation Overview

1. Preprocess your law data (chunk by section, ~300–500 words each).
2. Generate embeddings for each chunk using sentence-transformers or OpenAI embeddings.
3. Store embeddings in FAISS or ChromaDB.
4. In your /generate route:
    Embed the user’s query.
    Retrieve top-k similar chunks.
    Pass them as context to your model.
5. Add citation logic → attach source section names (e.g., “Section 5, Enforcement of Shari’ah Act”).
