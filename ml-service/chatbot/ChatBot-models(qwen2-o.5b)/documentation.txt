✅ When You Should Use supreme courts judgements 

If your goal is to build a Legal Urdu (or bilingual) advisory model, then yes — you should absolutely use them.

They are gold for:

Legal reasoning (learning how judges argue, cite, and conclude).

Legal language style (formal Urdu/English hybrid).

Context understanding (how law applies to real cases).

Grounding for advice generation (model learns what is legally correct, not just what “sounds right”).

If your future chatbot will:

Explain court decisions,

Provide summaries of judgments,

Or assist in legal document drafting,

then these documents are extremely valuable training data — especially after some preprocessing.

🧩 What happened

You fine-tuned Qwen/Qwen2-0.5B (base), which is the raw language model.
That model isn’t trained for chat or instruction following — it just continues text.
So when you ask “hello” or “what is property law?”, it doesn’t know that it should respond conversationally.
That’s why you get long, irrelevant stories or summaries.

For chatbots, you always want to start from a model that already understands dialogue format and instruction style.
